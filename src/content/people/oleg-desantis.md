Oleg leads our product engineering service. He has more than 15 years in the
software industry, has led quality and platform engineering teams, built and
coached test automation practices, and helped teams adopt DORA and SPACE to
understand how well they deliver.

He speaks about delivery strategy and leading indicators, including at
FastFlowConf and Domain-Driven Design London. His FastFlowConf 2025 talk, “A
Ferrari with No GPS”, argued that faster pipelines can’t fix a flawed strategy.

In it he shared the Triple Diamond Initiative Playbook, a map that takes an
initiative from a rough idea to running software, so the whole team can see
the value stream in one place. The playbook and his writing on engineering
leadership are at daowolf.com.
