practice: product-engineering

summary: >-
  Agree the early signals that show whether you’re building the right thing,
  before the results arrive.

  Your delivery metrics look healthy, but the business outcomes you care about
  aren’t following.

  - A facilitated workshop with leadership and delivery teams
  - Mapping your goals to the leading indicators that predict them
  - A lightweight playbook for tracking and acting on those signals

  Earlier, clearer signals, and the confidence to change course before it
  becomes costly.
