---
title: Test Automation Accelerator
practice: product-engineering
order: 5
summary: >-
  Build or rescue test automation your team trusts, so releases stop waiting on
  manual checks.
problem: >-
  Automated tests are slow, flaky or missing, so releases still depend on
  manual checks.
included:
  - An automation strategy that puts the right tests at the right levels
  - Framework design, or a rescue plan for what you already have
  - Pairing with your engineers to build the first suites
  - Practices that hold up whether people or AI agents write the code, such as
    mutation testing and behaviour-driven development
  - Integration with your delivery pipeline and reporting
outcome: >-
  Fast, reliable feedback from automation your team trusts and can extend.
