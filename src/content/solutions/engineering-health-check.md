---
title: Engineering Health Check
practice: product-engineering
order: 4
featured: true
summary: >-
  An independent review of how you plan, build, test and release software,
  with a prioritised plan to improve.
problem: >-
  Releases feel slow or risky, and you’re not sure where time and quality are
  being lost.
included:
  - Conversations with engineers, testers, product and delivery leads
  - A review of your pipelines, test suites, release data and delivery metrics
  - A look at how code written by people and AI agents is reviewed and tested,
    and whether tests check behaviour or implementation
  - Findings presented to your leadership team
  - A prioritised improvement roadmap
outcome: >-
  A shared, evidence-based view of where you are today and what to fix first.
---
